Purpose of Directionary

Directionary is a mobile app that helps people with visual impairments discover, learn and share routes through directions, clues and landmarks contributed by the community.

A supplementary tool

Directionary is a supplementary route-learning and wayfinding tool. It does not replace:

  • orientation and mobility training;
  • white canes, guide dogs or other mobility aids;
  • awareness of your surroundings;
  • your own judgement; or
  • assistance from other people when you need it.

Directionary is not a real-time navigation system and does not detect obstacles.

Community-contributed routes

Routes are contributed by users. They may be incomplete or become outdated, and may not reflect current conditions such as construction, weather or changes to a building or street. Directionary does not guarantee that any route is accurate or safe.

Your responsibilities

Use routes as one source of information. Stay aware of your surroundings, use the mobility skills and aids that suit you, and take appropriate precautions.
