Accessibility feedback

Directionary is designed for screen reader use, and we want to hear about anything that gets in the way. If you find an accessibility barrier or a problem using Directionary, please email directionary@guidedogs.org.sg. Tell us what you were trying to do, the device and screen reader you use, and what happened.
