Features

Directionary helps people discover, record and learn routes shared by the community.

  • Map Library

    Discover and explore routes contributed by the community, and find journeys relevant to you.

  • Create Routes

    Record routes using directions, clues, and landmarks. Share what you know so others can learn from it.

  • Learn Routes

    Review route information to build familiarity before travelling, and return to routes to reinforce what you have learned.

How routes are described

Directionary uses a framework informed by orientation and mobility (O&M) practices. Each component helps describe a different aspect of a journey.

Directions
Where do I move? Instructions that guide each step of a route.
Clues
How do I recognise my surroundings? Sensory and environmental information that helps identify where I am.
Landmarks
What can I orient myself by? Recognisable reference points along a route.

A guide, not a replacement

Directionary complements orientation and mobility training and existing mobility skills. Community-contributed routes may become outdated or inaccurate and do not guarantee safe navigation.

Directionary does not provide real-time obstacle detection. Always remain aware of your surroundings and exercise personal judgement.
