Features

Directionary helps people discover, record and learn routes shared by the community.

  • Map Library

    Discover and explore routes contributed by the community, and find journeys relevant to you.

  • Create Routes

    Record routes using directions, clues and landmarks, and share what you know so that others can learn from it.

  • Learn Routes

    Review route information to build familiarity before travelling, and return to a route to reinforce what you have learned.

How routes are described

Routes use an O&M-aligned framework. Each part answers a different question a traveller might have.

Directions
Where do I move? Instructions describing where to move.
Clues
How do I recognise where I am? Environmental and sensory information that helps someone recognise their surroundings.
Landmarks
What can I orient myself by? Recognisable reference points that help someone orient themselves.

A guide, not a replacement

Directionary is a supplementary route-learning and wayfinding tool. Routes are contributed by the community and may become outdated or not reflect current conditions.

Directionary complements orientation and mobility training and existing mobility skills. It does not replace them, does not provide real-time obstacle detection and does not guarantee route accuracy or safety. Read the Terms of Use
