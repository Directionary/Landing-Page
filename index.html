How it works

A little knowledge goes a long way.

Every journey begins with understanding where you’re going.

Directionary makes it easier to access and share the route knowledge that helps people familiarise themselves with their surroundings.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Explore routes shared by the community and find journeys relevant to you.

  2. 02

    Learn

    Understand directions, clues and landmarks to build familiarity with a route before travelling.

  3. 03

    Contribute

    Share your knowledge by creating routes that others can discover and learn from.

Our philosophy

Designed with purpose.

Directionary is guided by three principles that shape how we approach accessibility, clarity and independence.

  1. 01

    Ear First, Eyes Second

    We prioritise experiences that can be understood beyond what is seen.

    By designing for screen readers first, we aim to make information and interactions clear, meaningful and accessible.

  2. 02

    Less but Clear

    Simplicity should never come at the expense of clarity.

    We remove unnecessary complexity while ensuring people have the information they need to understand each interaction.

  3. 03

    Empower, Not Replace

    Directionary complements orientation and mobility training rather than replacing it.

    Our goal is to make shared route knowledge more accessible, encouraging exploration, familiarity and independence.

Our mission

Knowledge shared. Confidence built.

Learning a new route often involves understanding directions, recognising landmarks and remembering the details that make a journey familiar.

Directionary was created to make this knowledge easier to access and share.

By bringing community-contributed route information together, we hope to support people with visual impairments as they explore and familiarise themselves with new environments.

We believe technology should complement existing skills, not replace them.

About Directionary

A different approach to wayfinding.

Directionary began as a design initiative exploring how route knowledge could be made more accessible to people with visual impairments.

The project focuses on supporting route learning through community-contributed directions, clues and landmarks.

Directionary is developed in collaboration with Guide Dogs Singapore.

Responsible wayfinding

A guide, not a replacement.

Directionary is a supplementary route-learning and wayfinding tool.

Routes may contain community-contributed information that becomes outdated or does not reflect current environmental conditions. Directionary does not provide real-time obstacle detection or guarantee route accuracy or safety.

Users should continue to exercise personal judgement, remain aware of their surroundings and use appropriate mobility skills and aids.
