How it works

Directionary is community-driven. People with visual impairments do not only use routes; they create and share them too.

  1. Create

    People record routes they know, using directions, clues and landmarks.

  2. Share

    Routes are shared with the community so that others can find them.

  3. Learn

    Others review a route’s information to build familiarity before they travel.

Our approach

Directionary draws on orientation and mobility (O&M) principles. Routes are structured using directions, clues and landmarks.

Directions
Instructions describing where to move.
Clues
Environmental and sensory information that helps someone recognise their surroundings.
Landmarks
Recognisable reference points that help someone orient themselves.

Directionary complements orientation and mobility training and existing mobility skills. It does not replace them, does not provide real-time obstacle detection and does not guarantee route accuracy or safety. Read the Terms of Use

About Directionary

Directionary began as a design initiative exploring how route knowledge could be made more accessible to people with visual impairments. It is developed in collaboration with Guide Dogs Singapore.
