Record once, follow with confidence

Every route is an ordered list of steps. Each step carries a direction, a sensory clue, and a landmark — so the route still makes sense when you cannot see the street.

  • Sensory clues

    Attach what you will hear, smell or feel to every step — traffic noise, a bakery, a change in temperature. That is what makes a route followable without sight.

  • Landmarks that anchor you

    Point each step at a physical feature near you, picked from nearby places or typed in. No coordinates, no guesswork at the kerb.

  • A library of trusted routes

    Save your own routes and browse routes shared by others. Copy a community route and adapt it instead of starting from scratch.

  • Step-by-step walkthrough

    Follow one clear instruction at a time while you walk, with announcements built for screen readers.

  • Voice input

    Dictate directions, clues and landmarks instead of typing them — authoring works the way talking does.

  • Private by default

    Sign in with an email link, unlock with biometrics, and keep your tokens in secure on-device storage.

How it works

  1. 1

    Record a route

    Name an origin and a destination, then add one step at a time: direction, clue, landmark.

  2. 2

    Save it

    A route with no steps has nothing to follow, so nothing saves until the steps are in.

  3. 3

    Walk it

    Open the route, follow each step in order, and trust what you hear, smell and feel.

Accessibility is the product

Directionary is designed with screen readers first: every screen carries semantic labels, walkthrough steps are announced as you go, and text is written to be heard — not just read. If anything in the app blocks your travel, the in-app report form reaches the team directly.
