Privacy Policy — Directionary App (Google Play)

How the Directionary Android app handles personal information. Use this URL in Play Console.

Draft, not in force

This is a working draft for store review, grounded in the current Flutter + Firebase build. It has not had legal review and items marked “To be confirmed” must be confirmed before release. Questions: directionary@guidedogs.org.sg.

Summary

Directionary is a community-driven route-learning app for people with visual impairments. You create an account, browse community routes, and contribute routes with directions, clues and landmarks.

At a glance, the Android app handles:

  • account identifiers to sign you in (email + password and email sign-in link via Firebase Authentication);
  • content you provide (display name, routes, photos, feedback) stored in Firebase and shared with other users where you publish it;
  • optional device capabilities you grant: precise foreground location, camera / photos, microphone for voice input, notifications, biometric unlock;
  • on-device preferences and tokens, plus release-only crash reports and Firebase analytics.

We do not sell personal data and use no third-party advertising SDKs.

Who is responsible

The legal operator and data controller under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). Directionary is developed in collaboration with Guide Dogs Singapore; this page does not state who the legal operator is.

Data the app collects

Permissions are optional. Declining limits only the related feature. There is no background location tracking.

  • Account: email address, password (transmitted to Firebase Auth, never stored in clear), display name / username, Firebase UID and email-verified flag. Sign-in is email + password and email link (password reset by email). There is no SMS OTP and no Google / Apple sign-in in the current build.
  • Routes you contribute: origin / destination, descriptions, per-step directions, clues and landmarks, route geometry you author (lat / lng), public vs personal flag, timestamps, owner UID. Published (non-personal) routes are readable by anyone, including signed-out users; personal routes are owner-only. Attribution is by UID only; there is no public profile with display name.
  • Stars, reports, questions: star counts (public) with per-user star records; bug reports (reasons, description, optional map reference, reporter UID); FAQ questions (request text, reporter UID). Author-only or console-only reads as applicable.
  • Photos (optional): images you attach to routes (JPEG / PNG / WebP / GIF / HEIF, under 10 MB), stored in Firebase Storage. Camera on-device use is otherwise limited to QR scanning.
  • Precise location (permission: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, foreground only): single fix per use to show the map, find nearby points of interest and record route geometry you author. Not collected in the background. Current location is sent to Google Maps Places (Nearby Search / Find Place) for results and cached in memory for 60 seconds only.
  • Microphone (permission: RECORD_AUDIO): only for voice input you choose; audio is processed to provide transcription. No server-side voice store.
  • Notifications (permission: POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Firebase Cloud Messaging notification title / body shown via local notifications.
  • Biometric unlock (permission: USE_BIOMETRIC): verified on-device by the OS only; biometric data never leaves your device and is never sent to us.
  • Support: messages, bug / accessibility feedback and device details (model, OS, screen reader, app version) you include when you contact us.
  • Device and diagnostics: on-device preferences and auth session in secure storage (login state, search history up to 10 terms, saved routes, onboarding flags, lockout counters); release-only Firebase Crashlytics reports (error + stack trace); Firebase Analytics app-instance and usage data (automatic screen reporting is off; no custom events are wired in the current build).
To be confirmed

Final auth methods at launch, full Analytics event list if any are added, and FCM token handling. Confirm against the release build and mirror in the Play Data safety form.

How we use data

  • provide, maintain and secure accounts, routes and the Map Library;
  • display routes you publish to the community;
  • respond to support, accessibility feedback and bug reports;
  • keep the app stable and safe (crash diagnosis, abuse prevention);
  • understand aggregate use to improve accessibility.

We process your data with your consent and to perform the service you request, in line with the PDPA.

Sharing and third-party providers

We share data only as needed to run the app. Providers process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics) — account, content, messaging, crash and usage data; Firestore in asia-southeast1 (Singapore).
  • Google Maps / Play services (Maps SDK, Places API, location services) — map display and, with your permission, current location and search queries. Subject to Google’s privacy policy.
  • On-device services — speech recognition, biometric check, secure storage; data stays on your device unless you submit it (e.g. transcribed text in a route).

No sale of personal data. No third-party advertising SDKs.

To be confirmed

Confirm Storage bucket region (target asia-southeast1, fixed at first bucket write) and final processor list before release.

Storage, retention and security

App data is stored in Firebase (Singapore region) and on your device (secure storage for session and preferences). We use transport encryption, Firebase security rules and access controls, and limit access to people who need it.

To be confirmed

Retention schedule for accounts, routes, photos (note: deleting a route record currently orphans its Storage photos, removable via console only), reports, logs and backups. Must be confirmed before release and match the Data safety form.

Your rights and choices

  • access, correct or withdraw consent for your personal data by emailing directionary@guidedogs.org.sg;
  • manage permissions any time in Android Settings; declining location, camera, microphone or notifications limits only the related feature;
  • edit or unpublish routes you contributed from within the app where available, or by contacting us;
  • contact us about analytics / crash reporting.

We respond in line with the PDPA.

Account and data deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated personal data, email directionary@guidedogs.org.sg from your account email with the subject “Delete my Directionary account”. See also Support — Account deletion.

To be confirmed

There is currently no in-app account-deletion flow (in-app delete clears only the local session, not the Firebase account). Before release, confirm whether in-app deletion will be added, what is deleted vs retained (e.g. anonymised published routes, safety logs), verification steps and timeframe. Google Play requires this to be accurate and actionable.

Children

Directionary is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will post updates on this page and update the effective date below. Material changes will be notified in the app or by email where appropriate.

Contact

Data protection questions, access / correction / withdrawal requests, and deletion requests: directionary@guidedogs.org.sg.
